About Section

Title:
The VIRAMORÉ Story

Content:

True luxury isn't manufactured.
It is distilled through time.

Our journey led us to Kannauj — the heart of Indian perfumery for over 400 years.

Using the traditional Deg-Bhapka steam distillation process, we extract the purest essences of Oud, Amber, and Rose.

Each drop is distilled through patience and fire in copper stills.

This is not fragrance.

This is liquid legacy.

Vision
